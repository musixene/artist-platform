# Private feedback and data controls

Likes record current preference, not listening evidence. Notes to Artist are private plain text visible only to their author and authorized artist administrators; there is no public discussion, reply service or notification delivery. Do not include sensitive information you do not want the artist's administrators to read.

Listeners may like/unlike published tracks and submit multiple immutable notes of up to 2,000 Unicode characters each. Notes have retry identifiers to prevent duplicate submissions after a lost response. Server-side sessions, published-track checks, origin/CSRF validation and a persisted 20-change-per-minute allowance enforce write boundaries. Identical like/note retries do not use that allowance. No client-supplied user ID changes the author.

Unpublishing prevents new likes/notes and media access, but authors retain access to their own existing feedback through **My private feedback** and per-track own-data endpoints. Other listeners cannot read it. Disabling an account retains its data but denies access until re-enabled. Existing administrator bootstrap and last-administrator protections remain in force.

Authors can delete individual notes or all their own feedback, and export their own feedback in private JSON pages of up to 50 notes and 50 likes. Exports include pagination/counts; continue through every page for a full export. Pages reflect current data, not a frozen snapshot. No password/session hashes are exported. Administrators can filter by track/listener, inspect current likes and notes, remove notes without editing author text, and export matching pages. Administrator removals and export actions are audited without note bodies; audit entries are pruned after 90 days on subsequent audit activity. Exports and deletion controls share the persisted feedback rate allowance to bound audit growth. Export files are private and should be protected.

Notes and likes remain until explicitly deleted. Own deletion immediately removes feedback from the live database; other users' feedback and all music remain intact. Deleting an account through an authorized operator workflow must delete its feedback too (foreign keys enforce cascades). This release offers feedback deletion, not self-service deletion of the entire login account. Coordinate full account deletion with the instance operator. Disabled accounts are not automatically deleted.

Instance operators control backups and must publish an appropriate backup expiry schedule and private export handling policy before inviting listeners. Deleted feedback may remain in restricted backups until expiry; a restoration must reconcile intervening deletion requests before reopening access. No finite backup expiry is promised by the software. Do not copy feedback into source control or unrelated development installations. No external tracker, message delivery, or centralized Musixene account is required.

Schema 3 adds feedback tables/indexes, rate state and a minimal privacy audit log in an atomic migration, leaving existing accounts, sessions, catalog, collections and media intact. Back up the complete stopped instance before upgrading. Downgrades require matching code and a complete pre-upgrade restore, not a schema edit or overlay. Version-1 preservation, schema-3 failure/retry and populated feedback archive restore are automated tests. Credits remain disabled. Playback analytics is a separately reviewed follow-up; this release does not collect listening duration.
